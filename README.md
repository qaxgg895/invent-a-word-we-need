## The entry

One coined word with a one-line definition, 93 characters:

> Corridance — the awkward dance of two people in a hallway who keep sidestepping the same way.

*Corridance* (corridor + dance) names the little mirrored shuffle
everyone has done and nobody had a word for: two people approaching
each other in a hallway, each stepping aside, each stepping the same
way again, until one of them finally laughs and waves the other
through. The gap is real — English has phrases for it ("the hallway
dance") but no single word — and the definition lands in one reading:
the scene is the definition. The word is built from plain English
parts, sayable at first sight (kor-ih-dance), and short enough to
stick. Web searches found no dictionary entry, slang listing, or any
other use of "corridance" — it is freshly coined here.
